# Privacy Policy for Amar Hishab

**Effective Date:** July 22, 2026

Amar Hishab ("the app") is a personal finance management application
developed by Md. Sayeed Rahman.

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use
the app.

## Overview

Amar Hishab is designed with privacy in mind.

We do **not collect, store, or access your personal financial information**.
Your financial records remain under your control and do not leave your
device unless you explicitly choose to use backup features.


## Information You Enter

Amar Hishab allows you to create and manage personal financial records,
including:

- Expenses
- Savings
- Investments
- Loans
- Other financial notes entered by you

This information is entered voluntarily by you to use the app features.


## Data Storage

Your financial data is stored locally on your device.

We do not upload, transmit, or store your financial data on our own servers.

The developer does not have access to your expenses, savings, investments,
loans, or other personal records.


## Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, your data is backed up directly
to your own Google Drive account.

Important points:

- Backup is controlled by you.
- Your backup data is stored in your personal Google Drive.
- We do not have access to your Google Drive files.
- We do not receive, view, or store your backup data on our servers.

Google Drive backup is provided only to help you restore your data on your
own devices.


## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or provide your personal financial information to
third parties.

Your financial records are never shared with advertisers or other companies.


## Third-Party Services

The app may use Google services for features such as Google Drive backup
(if enabled by you).

These services operate according to their own privacy policies.


## Security

Because your data is stored on your device and/or your personal Google Drive,
you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Google
account.

We take reasonable steps to design the app securely, but no digital storage
system can guarantee absolute security.


## Children's Privacy

Amar Hishab is not specifically designed for children under 13 years of age.


## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page.
